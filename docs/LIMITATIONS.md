# Scope, limitations and privacy

## Supported scope

A local, single-workspace undergraduate document guide for FAST-NUCES and selected CFD services. It explains retrieved evidence, shows source pages and saves conversations. It is student-built, not an official decision system.

## Evidence and answers

- Only reviewed sections are indexed. Excluded postgraduate/external-applicant rules cannot be inferred from undergraduate rules; they may still exist elsewhere in the complete PDFs.
- Snapshots cannot confirm recent announcements, current availability, personal bills or official student records.
- Documents can conflict or contain ambiguous wording. The chatbot cannot choose official precedence or guarantee a decision.
- Retrieval budgets, bounded history, PDF/OCR artifacts and model mistakes may omit or misinterpret conditions.
- Valid citation IDs do not prove semantic support. Targeted guards and source-backed arithmetic protect known families, not every future claim or calculation.
- No finite test suite guarantees 100% accuracy. Independent high-impact policy review remains pending; important decisions require university confirmation.

## Operations

Google chat/query embeddings need internet and quota. Same-project keys can share limits. Model availability and free-tier eligibility can change. There is no paid/provider fallback, but a billed key can incur charges: the app does not enforce the provider's billing settings.

Full transcripts persist, but active policy context uses only six completed exchanges within a token budget. Old answers may predate document changes; saved citations are records of the original answer, not current-policy certification.

## Privacy and security boundaries

Keys stay in backend `.env`; relevant questions/history/evidence go to Google. Local databases and diagnostics are not encrypted and have no user accounts. Do not submit sensitive personal records. Loopback binding, host/origin checks, request validation, safe rendering and sanitized errors serve the local scope; they are not public multiuser security qualification. Do not expose the local port publicly.

The repository excludes personal chat storage, API keys, vector caches, dependencies, builds and raw evaluation transcripts. Only the four approved reference PDFs are intentionally bundled. Their inclusion does not imply university endorsement or a new open-content license. Preserve source provenance and review future replacements.
